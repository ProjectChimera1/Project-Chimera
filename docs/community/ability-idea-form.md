# ⚗️ Chimera Ability Workshop — pitch us an ability!

Fill in what you know, skip what you don't. One sentence per line is plenty.
Wild ideas are welcome — even "impossible" ones tell us what to build next.

---

**1. Name it:**
*(e.g., "Blood Tithe")*

**2. The pitch — what happens, in one sentence:**
*(e.g., "Sacrifice your own health to melt everything around you.")*

**3. Cast it, or always on?**
*(Active = you click it. Passive = it just happens. If passive: when does it kick in — always on / when my attacks land / every few seconds / when I die?)*

**4. Who does it affect?**
*(Just me / one ally / one enemy / a spot on the ground / everyone near me)*

**5. Play-by-play — step by step, rough numbers welcome:**
*(e.g., "Pay 40 HP → deal 80 damage to every enemy within 6m → survivors are slowed 25% for 3 seconds")*

**6. The price:**
*(Mana? Your own health? Long cooldown? Limited charges? Free but weak?)*

**7. Look & sound — what do you SEE and HEAR when it goes off?**
*(e.g., "a red transmutation circle flashes, deep bell toll, tiny screen shake")*

**8. Who carries it?**
*(What kind of unit or hero is this for — e.g., "a slow tank hero," "a cheap swarm unit," "a healer")*

**9. Break it for us — the cheesiest abuse you can think of. Be evil:**
*(e.g., "I'd stack 12 of them and suicide-bomb your economy")*

**10. Seen something like it before?** *(optional)*
*(Any game/ability we should look at for reference)*

### Example (so you can see the effort bar — this took 2 minutes)

1. **Name:** Rust Plague
2. **Pitch:** A corrosion curse that eats armored units alive and spreads when they die.
3. **Cast or passive:** Active, cast on one enemy.
4. **Affects:** One enemy, then jumps to others.
5. **Play-by-play:** Target takes 5 damage/sec for 8s → if it dies infected, the plague jumps to the 2 nearest mechanical enemies.
6. **Price:** 50 energy, 20s cooldown.
7. **Look & sound:** Orange rust crawls over the model, dry crackling like breaking pottery.
8. **Carrier:** A mid-tier support caster.
9. **Break it:** Feed it cheap units on purpose so it chain-jumps through the whole army for one cast.
10. **Reference:** Plague from WC3 undead.
