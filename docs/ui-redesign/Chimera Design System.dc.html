Design system · 05
Ornament and motion
Every lever: off, then 1–5
Motion levers have separate speed and strength. Master intensity scales them all: level × master ÷ 2. Default master 2, every lever 2.
Player option: Off · Subtle · Full
In Settings › Graphics and Settings › Accessibility. Off keeps plain borders; Subtle caps every lever at 2; Full uses the Architect’s levels.
Reduced motion
Stops all motion and leaves the ornaments still. {{ lvNotes.rm }}
Limits
Motion never moves text or controls, never flashes, and never loops faster than every 2.4 s. In a match it stays on edges and alerts. No blur, no particles.
