== Reading (main-session context; not a gate)
- "algo=17" in the chmr line is CanonicalModelHash.AlgoVersion (the replay header field), not SimChecksum's algo 29.
- What A14 shows: the real MainScene (every presentation system live, PathRequestSystem's frame-paced Move->Stop
  writer included) reproduces the golden on every tick at two different frame pacings (interval 60: ~47 fps, 262-285
  multi-step frames; interval 1: 547 frames, 377 multi-step frames), and the wide digest, which folds the unfolded
  CommandState that writer moves (plan A F40), equals the trailer at 300/900/1440.
- What it does not show: that DW-681 is harmless in general. trial_1000's only plain Move orders (cmd 1) are 60 rows
  for the SAME 30 units (tick 150 and again at tick 300), and at tick 300 each of those units also gets a queued
  AttackMove (cmd 0x82). By the code (not instrumented), the sim's queue therefore resolves their arrival, so no unit
  sits in Move with an empty queue at its goal, which is the state DW-681's writer acts on. DW-681 stays open as filed;
  a scenario with an un-queued Move that reaches its goal would be needed to exercise it.
- The .chmr spans several merged frames per tick: ReplayRecorder's 8 x 32 = 256-orders-per-tick envelope cannot hold
  the trial's 960 tick-0 orders, so ChmrTool writes the recorder's exact v8 header and the frozen MergedTickPacket codec
  directly, in CSV order (decode check 2077/2077; managed control 1440/1440 on both variants).
