## Story

As a player,
I want armies to finish fights against buildings, never freeze on arrival, and long matches to stay stable,
So that DestroyAllBuildings is winnable by anyone (including the AI) and late-game has no structural glitches.

## Why this matters (source-verified at HEAD 9b85f85)

**Four confirmed bug-class findings, no prior owner, bundled as the Epic-2-closing defect batch** (gap-analysis Gaps #59 + #60, MAJOR; the 2026-06-09 utility-AI-deadlock investigation Mechanisms 3+4; deferred-work #7 and §2.2b/§2.6/§2.10). Each is a **pure sim-layer fix, deterministic, with its own negative/regression test.** They are independent — no shared fold serializes them — but they interact at two seams (building *death* feeds both the win condition and the recycle free-list; recycling makes the exact building-index holds that defect #1 adds more of), so they ship together with the interactions made explicit.

**Defect #1 — attack-move/idle units never auto-acquire buildings; the AI never razes; DestroyAllBuildings can't conclude.** Story 2.9a deliberately made anti-building combat **explicit-order-only** — the comment at `CombatSystem.cs:283-284` reads verbatim *"Explicit-order-only: nothing auto-acquires buildings."* Every auto-acquisition state (`TickIdleCombat:156-190`, `TickAttackMoveCombat:436-466`, `TickStationaryCombat:206-233`, `TickPatrolCombat:330-359`) calls only `SpatialHash.FindNearestEnemy`/`FindNearestEnemyGlobal` (`:74-124`/`:131-157`), and `SpatialHash.Rebuild` (`:34-68`) **populates the hash from `EntityWorld` entities ONLY — buildings are never in the combat spatial index.** The one building-search that exists, `SelectionSystem.FindNearestEnemyBuilding:908-926`, is **presentation-only** (Godot `Vector3`, float math, `Faction.Player1` hardcoded) — sim-illegal and non-deterministic, **not reusable**. On the AI side, `AiOpponentSystem.DoLaunchAttack:327-342` flips its wave to `AttackMove` aimed at a **hardcoded `P1_BASE = (-45,0,0)`** (`:50`) and **never issues `AttackBuilding`** (grep-confirmed: 0 hits). So an AI assault that kills all defenders then stands **inert** next to enemy structures forever (deferred #7: *"arrived waves have nothing to do at an enemy base anyway"*). Buildings **do** die today — `DamageResolver.ApplyToBuilding:88-101` (the single shared building-damage entry, reached by melee `TryDealBuildingDamage` and ranged `ProjectileSystem.ApplyBuildingHit`) subtracts Health, pushes `BuildingDestroyed`, and calls `BuildingStore.Destroy` at ≤0 HP — so **the win state is reachable the moment armies actually attack buildings.** The gap is purely acquisition + AI issuance; the win check itself (`MainScene.CheckWinCondition:1152-1189`, `DestroyAllBuildings:1158-1171` = "a faction has zero Alive buildings") already polls `BuildingStore.Alive` every frame and concludes real matches.

**Defect #2 — the AttackMove arrival-hover deadlock (deferred #7).** `AMOVE_ARRIVE_SQR` (`CombatSystem.cs:69`, a **0.5u radius**, not `:38` and not "0.5u²" as the stale doc says) is the sole AttackMove→Idle threshold, tested in `ResumeAttackMove:472-485` (`:475`). A wave sharing one `CommandGoal` **never reaches it**: `MovementSystem` seek scales linearly to zero near the goal (`SLOW_RADIUS=4.0`, `speed *= dist/SLOW_RADIUS`, `:22,:87-88`) while separation stays strong (`SEPARATION_STRENGTH=2.5`, `:31`, contact = summed collision radii, run on **all** alive units `:93-126`), so the wave settles on an **equilibrium ring at ~1.0u** where seek balances separation — `SqrDistance(Position,CommandGoal)` never drops to 0.5u, `ResumeAttackMove` never fires →Idle, and the units hover in `AttackMove` forever, never returning to the AI's Idle/Stop pool to be re-waved. **Confirmed in current code.** Three sibling thresholds are all 0.5u and share the crowd-unreachability: `AMOVE_ARRIVE_SQR` (`CombatSystem.cs:69`), `MovementSystem.ARRIVE_THRESHOLD_SQR` (`:18-19`), and **`OrderQueueSystem.ORDER_ARRIVE_SQR` (`:35`, consumed `:72`)** — so a queued Move behind a wave, or a wave of queued Moves to one point, strands identically. Story 2.12's R1 `FlowFieldBridge` handoff (`:165`) does **not** cure this — it is Move-only, single-unit, and orthogonal to the separation ring, and it explicitly defers AttackMove arrival to the sim (`FlowFieldBridge.cs:175`). **The fix must apply consistently to all three thresholds** or queued/waved movement still strands.

**Defect #3 — BuildingStore is 64-slot, append-only, never recycled (Gap #60).** `MAX_BUILDINGS=64` (`BuildingStore.cs:25`); `Count` is a monotonic high-water mark (`:62`, `Count++` at `:92`) and `Create` returns -1 once 64 have **ever** been placed (`:90`), even if all are dead. `Destroy` (`:156-160`) only sets `Alive[id]=false` and **leaks the slot** ("slot is not reused in Phase 1"). The 64 is the cumulative lifetime total across **all factions** — rebuilds, expansions, and Epic-9 4-player matches (4×~20 buildings) blow past it, after which **every placement silently fails for the rest of the match** (`PlaceBuildingDirect:416-424` returns -1; `QueueWorkerBuild:485-518` refunds ore; the AI build actions refund). Story 2.12 already reset `RallyPoint`/`HasRallyPoint` in `Create()` (`:101-106`) with a comment explicitly citing *"forward-safe for the Story 2.13 slot recycling."* The fix is a **deterministic free-list** mirroring the in-repo `ProjectileStore._freeList` (a LIFO stack, unfolded). **The #1 risk:** Story 2.9a's AttackBuilding crash-safety rests on *"slots never reused → a stale `CommandTarget` resolves to `!Alive` → clean revert"* — recycling breaks that invariant (a stale index resolves to `Alive` again, a *new* building). This is **not a desync and not a crash** (recycling is deterministic; every deref bounds-checks `< Count`; a recycled index is always `< Count`; the `TickAttackBuildingCombat` guard `:295-297` re-validates `Alive`+`FactionOf` every tick → friendly/dead recycle reverts cleanly), but it silently violates "clean revert" → an ABA mis-target onto a *new enemy* building at the reused slot. Story 2.13 **armors** this (Decision D-3) with a per-slot generation counter + packed building-refs (`(gen<<8)|slot`, validated on deref), restoring the clean-revert contract — golden-neutral since generation starts at 0.

**Defect #4 — the lifelong Sanguine Furnace HoT dies at the 256-pulse cap (§2.6/§2.10 furnace risk).** A `while_alive` self-passive is installed once at spawn (`AbilityCastSystem.InstallSelfPassive:147`, via `EntityWorld.OnUnitDefinitionApplied`). `ModifierStore.InstallPersistent:189` clamps its pulses to `EffectCaps.MaxPersistentPeriods=256` (`:207-210`) and `Advance:226` expires the persistent the instant `_periodsRemaining` hits 0 (`:257-258` → `RemoveSlot:269`), with **nothing re-installing it**. `furnace_trickle.json` (heal 3 / `period_ticks 15` / `period_count 256`) therefore stops regenerating at **3840 ticks = 128s**; `furnace_pour.json` (heal 6 / 15 / 256) likewise. **The "256" is `EffectCaps.MaxPersistentPeriods`, a clamp constant — NOT a byte and NOT an array bound** (`_periodsRemaining` is already `int[]`, `:54`; the only ring bound is `MaxModifiersPerEntity=8`, `:70`) — so the "byte→int counter widening" framing is a misdiagnosis. Authoring cannot exceed 256 (`period_count:999999` validates then silently clamps at `:208`); `period_ticks` is the only data lever, and stretching it wrecks the 2.10 heal cadence. The correct fix is an **in-place re-arm on expiry** (refill the *same* slot's already-folded `_periodsRemaining`/`_ticksUntilPeriod`), never a re-`InstallPersistent` (which has **no same-id dedup**, `:189` vs `Apply:130-134` — a second install would stack a concurrent HoT and, after 8, exhaust the 8-slot ring).

## Acceptance Criteria

### AC1 — Attack-move/idle units auto-acquire enemy buildings; the AI razes; a headless match on DestroyAllBuildings concludes (epic AC1)

**Given** an attack-moving or idle combat unit with no enemy units in acquisition range but an enemy building in range **When** acquisition runs **Then** the unit acquires and attacks the building (`AttackBuilding` path), and the AI issues building attacks when no units remain **And** a headless AI-vs-AI match on DestroyAllBuildings CONCLUDES with a winner instead of stalling.

- **AC1.1** A new **deterministic sim-side building search** on `CombatSystem` (`Fixed` math, ascending-id, linear O(≤64) scan of the already-threaded `_buildings` store; `SpatialHash` has **no** building index and `SelectionSystem.FindNearestEnemyBuilding` is presentation-only float — do NOT reuse it) returns the nearest **in-range, alive, enemy, Structure-attackable** building for an entity: filter `Alive[b]`, `FactionOf[b] != world.FactionOf[i]`, `(world.AttackDomainOf[i] & AttackDomain.Structure) != None`, and `FixedVec3.SqrDistance(Position[i], _buildings.Position[b]) <= AttackRange[i]²`, tie-broken by ascending id (never a float distance).
- **AC1.2** **Idle** auto-acquisition: in `TickIdleCombat`, after `FindNearestEnemy` returns -1 (`:164`) and **before** the `FindNearestEnemyGlobal` chase (`:167`), if the new building search finds one, acquire it (Decision D-6 scopes this to **Idle + AttackMove only**; Stop/Hold/Patrol are out).
- **AC1.3** **AttackMove** auto-acquisition: in `TickAttackMoveCombat`, after `FindNearestEnemy` returns -1 (`:444`) and **before** `ResumeAttackMove` (`:448`), if the building search finds one, acquire it.
- **AC1.4** Acquisition **reuses `TickAttackBuildingCombat` verbatim** (no new combat body): set `world.CommandState[i] = UnitCommand.AttackBuilding; world.CommandTarget[i] = bId; world.AttackTarget[i] = -1;` (`AttackTarget[]` is entity-space and MUST NOT hold a building id — the explicit path sets it -1 for exactly this reason). The existing in-tick guard/chase/damage/revert (`:286-324`, guard `:295-297`) then drives it. Per Decision **D-2**, on razing the building the unit reverts to **Idle** (the guard's existing `→Idle` at `:299`), not back to AttackMove.
- **AC1.5** **AI issues `AttackBuilding` when no enemy units remain.** Add a fallback in `AiOpponentSystem` (extend `DoLaunchAttack:327-342` or a new `StrategicAction.RazeBuildings`): scan `_buildings` (template `BuildSnapshot:148-167`) for the nearest **enemy** (non-AI-faction) alive building; for each surviving available unit set `world.CommandState[i] = UnitCommand.AttackBuilding; world.CommandTarget[i] = bId;` (NOT `CommandGoal` — `TickAttackBuildingCombat` reads `world.CommandTarget[i]` at `:288`). Prefer Structure-domain units; non-Structure units self-revert (`:297`) harmlessly. **This is a behavior addition inside the existing float AI — float→Fixed conversion is OUT of scope** (Story 10.11 / MP-AI prerequisite, deferred-work:93; Decision baked).
- **AC1.6** A **headless Tier-1 regression test** proves the match reaches a winner. Because win-condition evaluation is **presentation-only** (`MainScene.CheckWinCondition`, Godot-side, Tier-1-unreachable — the `WinConditionSystem` the `ScenarioData:6-8` docstring names **does not exist**; the headless server evaluates no win condition), the test **re-implements the DestroyAllBuildings predicate** (`CountFactionBuildings(host, faction) == 0`, `AiActiveGoldenTests.cs:126-132`) and steps the `AiActiveScenario`/`GoldenHarness` host (`SimulationHost.StepOnce`) for a bounded tick budget, asserting the passive faction's alive-building count reaches 0 (Decision **D-5**). The assertion is a **state predicate, not a hash compare**, so the AI's float non-determinism does not block it (it runs headless on every OS). ("AI-vs-AI" is literally not constructible today — `AI_FACTION` is a hardcoded `Player2` const, `AiOpponentSystem.cs:31`; the operative repro is **P2-AI army razing a passive P1 base**, exactly the `AiActiveScenario` shape.)

### AC2 — AttackMove/queued-Move waves settle or engage on arrival instead of hovering, guarded by a Tier-1 regression test (epic AC2)

**Given** the known AttackMove arrival-hover defect reproduction scenario **When** the fixed build runs it **Then** units settle or engage instead of hovering indefinitely, guarded by a Tier-1 regression test.

- **AC2.1** The arrival test is fixed so a **crowded wave converging on one `CommandGoal` completes** (transitions AttackMove→Idle) instead of ringing at ~1.0u forever. Approach per Decision **D-1** (recommended: widen the arrive radius to a modest fixed value ~2u that clears the equilibrium ring for realistic wave sizes, composing with AC1's auto-acquire — an arrived unit that goes Idle 2u out then auto-acquires the base's in-range buildings; alternative: a no-progress-near-goal detector for arbitrary-scale robustness).
- **AC2.2** The fix is applied **consistently to all three 0.5u thresholds** or queued/waved movement still strands: `CombatSystem.AMOVE_ARRIVE_SQR` (`:69`, `ResumeAttackMove:475`), `MovementSystem.ARRIVE_THRESHOLD_SQR` (`:18-19`, `:75`), and `OrderQueueSystem.ORDER_ARRIVE_SQR` (`:35`, `:72`). Prefer a single shared named constant (or one per system pinned to the same value) so they cannot drift. Use the `Fixed.Half * Fixed.Half` idiom (as `OrderQueueSystem.cs:35` does) or `Fixed.FromInt`-based construction, **not** `Fixed.FromFloat` in a per-tick path (CHM0005 advisory; `AMOVE_ARRIVE_SQR`'s current `FromFloat` is a one-time static init, acceptable, but a new radius constant should follow the 2.12 clean idiom).
- **AC2.3** A **Tier-1 regression test** drives a wave (≥5 units) at one `CommandGoal` headless and asserts every unit reaches `CommandState == Idle` (or engages) within a bounded tick budget — RED on the pre-fix 0.5u radius, GREEN after. Because separation runs in pure sim (`MovementSystem`, no presentation), the headless test faithfully reproduces the equilibrium ring (unlike the queue path, which needs live path delegates — see Regression risks). A queued-Move-behind-a-wave case (or a wave of queued Moves) is included to cover `OrderQueueSystem.ORDER_ARRIVE_SQR`.
- **AC2.4** **Determinism disposition (stated):** changing the arrive radius alters *when* units flip to Idle → alters their `Position` trajectory (already folded) and `CommandState` timing (unfolded, transitive). **No new array, no AlgoVersion bump** (stays 9). Any existing golden whose wave converges on a goal **re-baselines** (prime suspects: `command-vocabulary-scenario`, `formation-separation-scenario`; audit each) — but **only** those whose observed behavior actually shifts, each with its disposition stated in the Dev Record, and each verified to move for the radius change **and nothing else** (the header stays `checksum_algo_version: 9`; a header flip or an unexplained sample move = a leaked fold/write → fix, don't re-baseline over it).

### AC3 — BuildingStore free-list recycling past 64 slots; recycled slots carry zero stale state; the count-driven checksum fold stays consistent (epic AC3)

**Given** a long match where more than 64 buildings are placed and destroyed sequentially **When** the free-list recycling added to `BuildingStore` reuses dead slots **Then** placement keeps working past the old 64-slot exhaustion, recycled slots carry zero stale state (the SoA-recycle trap test extended to `BuildingStore`), and the count-driven checksum fold stays consistent — goldens re-baselined ONLY if observed behavior actually shifts, with the disposition stated.

- **AC3.1** `BuildingStore` gains a **deterministic free-list** mirroring `ProjectileStore._freeList` (a LIFO `int[]` stack + `int _freeCount`, **unfolded** — internal bookkeeping, never a `SimChecksum` input). `Destroy(id)` (`:156-160`) pushes `id` onto the free-list (in addition to `Alive[id]=false`); `Create(...)` (`:88-153`) **pops** a free slot if available, else appends via `Count++` (the existing `Count >= MAX_BUILDINGS` guard now only trips when 64 slots are simultaneously *live*, not cumulatively). Recycled-index assignment is deterministic (LIFO from the deterministic tick), so all peers map slot→building identically.
- **AC3.2** **Every per-building field is reset on a recycled slot.** `Create()` is already a full initializer for 13 of 14 arrays; **close the one gap: `SupplyBonus` is not set in the `default:` switch branch (`:143-147`)** — zero it unconditionally before the switch (or in `default:`), or a `CommandCenter` (`SupplyBonus=10`) slot recycled into a `default`-typed building inherits stale +10 supply (the SoA-recycle trap). All *folded* building fields (`Alive`/`Health`/`ConstructionTimer`/`HasRallyPoint`/`RallyPoint`) are already reset (`:94,:112-146,:150,:105-106`), so recycling introduces **no checksum-visible stale state**.
- **AC3.3** The **SoA-recycle trap test is extended to `BuildingStore`** (mirroring `RecycledSlot_CarriesNoPrior*` for `EntityWorld`): place a building, `Destroy` it, `Create` a *different-typed* building into the reused slot, and assert every field (especially `SupplyBonus`, `RallyPoint`, `HasRallyPoint`, `ProductionQueue`, `TrainedCount`, `Health`) is the new building's value, never the prior occupant's. - **AC3.4** **The stale cross-tick building-index hazard is ARMORED** (Decision **D-3**, Alec): a per-slot `BuildingStore.Generation[]` (`int[]`, **unfolded**, incremented when a slot is recycled) + a **packed building-ref** `(generation << 8) | slot` carried in every cross-tick holder (`CommandTarget` under AttackBuilding, the AttackBuilding wire `TargetX`, `OrderQueueTargetX`, `ProjectileStore.TargetId`, the AI `_productionBuildingIds`/`_cmdCenterExpId`; `world.BuildTarget` too). Determinism-safe: `Generation` is deterministic + unfolded (transitive via folded `Alive`/`Count`) → **NO fold, AlgoVersion stays 9.** A teeth test proves a stale packed-ref (post-recycle generation mismatch) reverts, and a friendly/enemy recycle both resolve deterministically.
- **AC3.5** **Determinism disposition (stated):** the building fold is **count-driven and already folds `Alive[]`** (`SimChecksum.cs:210-225`, `0..Count`), the free-list reuses slots *below* `Count`, and the free-list bookkeeping stays unfolded → **no new folded state, no AlgoVersion bump (stays 9), all 17 goldens byte-identical** (no golden ever recycles past 64 — every golden places ≤ a handful of buildings and never destroys-then-places past cap). **Verdict to state in the Dev Record: no observed behavior shift, no re-baseline.** A new `building-recycle` golden/Tier-1 scenario pins the recycle path.
