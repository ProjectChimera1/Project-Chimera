## Intent

**Problem:** The Story 7.11 `WinConditionSystem` is hard-2-player: `EvaluateBuiltin`/`CountBuildingsAlive`/`CountUnitsAlive` count only `Player1`/`Player2`, and `OtherFaction` returns "the single other active faction," so a 3–4-faction match ignores Player3/Player4 and declares victory while they are still alive — the VERIFIED BLOCKER "3+ player matches cannot conclude" (DW-185). There is also no sim model of who is allied with whom, so a match can never resolve for "the last team standing."

**Approach:** Introduce a sim-owned **alliance mask** (a per-faction team id, default teams-of-1 = FFA) as a first-class folded sim store, and generalize `WinConditionSystem` from the 2-faction `OtherFaction` assumption to N-faction, team-aware resolution: every active faction is evaluated, defeated factions latch a per-player `VERDICT_LOST` at a deterministic tick while the match CONTINUES, and victory resolves for the **last team standing** (or the first team to satisfy a positive preset objective). Presentation replaces its P1/P2 game-over path with verdict consumption: a locally-eliminated player flips to the existing RevealAll spectator view with a defeat banner and keeps watching until the match ends.

## Boundaries & Constraints

**Always:**
- The alliance mask is **pure sim** state in a new store: per-faction team id, sized `FactionRegistry.SLOT_DEFINITIONS_SIZE` (5, Neutral+Player1..4), default **FFA** (team id = faction slot index — every faction its own team), `Clear()` restores the FFA default, owned as a `SimulationHost` property, reset from `ClearForReset()`. Expose `AreAllied(Faction, Faction)` and `TeamOf(Faction)`. Two factions are allied iff same team id; a faction is always allied with itself; `Neutral` (slot 0) is never a playable team member and never wins/loses. Populated from the lobby by Story 9.15 later — this story ships the model + the FFA default only.
- The alliance mask folds into `SimChecksum.Compute` — this is the story's **one** determinism bump: place its fold **immediately after the existing `WinStateStore` block and before the SimRng block**, iterating `factions.ActiveFactions` (ascending, never a raw `0..N` stride), one `Mix` per active faction (the team id). A **null** store folds byte-identically to a default-FFA store (`Mix((int)f)` per active faction). Bump `SimChecksum.AlgoVersion` 19→20 with a version note, **re-baseline every per-tick world golden** in the same commit, update the hand-pinned `SimChecksumCoverageGuardTest` known-state hash (v19→v20, copy the hex from the failure message, rename the method) and add a differential-mutation assertion that a changed team id moves the hash, and update `VersionStampConsistencyTests`.
- `WinConditionSystem` evaluates **every** `factions.ActiveFactions` member (never `Player1`/`Player2` literals). It takes the `AllianceStore` in its constructor. All existing purity rules hold: no `using Godot`, no `float`/`double`/`Mathf`/`Fixed.FromFloat`, no wall-clock; entities iterate `0..HighWaterMark` skipping `!IsAlive`; every value is an integer tick.
- **Per-player elimination:** a faction whose preset loss predicate is met latches `VERDICT_LOST` at the tick it is met (grace rules from 7.11 preserved for loss-by-absence), the verdict is final, and **the match continues** for every unresolved faction. Presentation reads `WinState` verdicts every frame (no win math): when the **local** faction (`LockstepManager.LocalFaction`, `Player1` offline) latches `VERDICT_LOST` and the match is not yet fully resolved, set `FogBridge.RevealAll = true` and show a non-terminal **defeat banner**, and keep ticking; only when the match fully resolves does `ShowGameOver` fire.
- **Last-team-standing victory:** after the per-tick loss pass, the set of "live" teams = teams with ≥1 active faction whose verdict is still `VERDICT_NONE`. If exactly one team is live **and** at least one faction has latched `VERDICT_LOST` (resolution has begun), latch `VERDICT_WON` for that team's still-live factions. On a tick that would eliminate the last ≥2 live teams **simultaneously**, apply a deterministic tie-break: the **highest-slot** faction among those eliminated this tick (and its team) wins instead — this preserves the 7.11 built-in parity where a P1+P2 double-elimination resolves to **Player2**.
- **Positive-objective presets win their whole team:** when King-of-the-Hill hold or Timed-Survival deadline is satisfied, latch `VERDICT_WON` for the satisfying faction's entire team and `VERDICT_LOST` for all other active factions.
- **Team-aware preset semantics** (all four 7.11 presets, verified headless with 3–4 factions):
  - *King of the Hill:* the sole-hold rule is by **team** — the counter advances only on a tick exactly one team has ≥1 alive unit in the region (allied co-occupants do not contest each other; a Neutral bystander neither holds nor contests), and resets when a team no longer solely holds. Cumulative hold accrues per team; win when a team reaches `hold_ticks`.
  - *Timed Survival:* the designated faction's **team** wins if the designated faction is alive at the deadline; if the designated faction is eliminated before the deadline (grace-gated) its whole team latches `VERDICT_LOST` and the remaining teams keep playing to a last-team-standing resolution.
  - *Assassination / Landmark:* the designated leader/structure belongs to one faction; its death (a resolved target, never grace-gated) latches `VERDICT_LOST` for that faction's whole **team**. Every other faction is eliminated by the standard total-wipeout rule (no alive units and no alive buildings, grace-gated), and the winner resolves by last-team-standing.
- **Built-in win conditions stay parity-correct in the 2-faction case:** a P1/P2 `DestroyAllBuildings`/`EliminateAllUnits` match must still name the same winner/loser as 7.11 (proven by the existing parity tests kept green), including the double-elimination→Player2 tie-break.
- Eliminated factions' remaining entities **remain in place under their own faction** (neither force-removed nor reassigned to Neutral) — the elimination is a verdict only; a latched-`LOST` faction can never subsequently win. Document this rule per preset.
- The `ScenarioDirector.OnVictory` trigger action stays intact as the advanced/T3 escape hatch. `WinConditionSystem` stays registered at index 14 (after `AiOpponentSystem`, immediately before `ScenarioDirector`); do not change the 16-system order.
- Close DW-185 in the deferred-work ledger on landing.

**Block If:**
- Making the new alliance-mask fold **re-save-neutral / handshake-safe is impossible** without a divergent hash on unchanged content beyond the single `AlgoVersion` bump — HALT rather than ship a divergent handshake.
- Satisfying an AC would require a **second** determinism bump (e.g. also folding the mask into `CanonicalModelHash`) or pulling **Story 9.14/9.15 lobby-team wiring** or **Story 7.13 per-faction instance-designation vocabulary** forward — HALT rather than expand scope.
- If the `SimChecksum.AlgoVersion` 19→20 integer collides with another in-flight story's bump on rebase, HALT (colliding-bump hazard) rather than silently reuse it.

**Never:**
- No **per-faction multi-target designation** — the preset data model keeps its single `leader_unit_index` / `structure_index` / `faction_slot`; do NOT add per-faction arrays of designated leaders/landmarks/survivors. Full symmetric multi-leader assassination for N FFA players needs either that data-model expansion or the Story 7.13 instance-designation DSL vocabulary, and is out of scope (documented boundary; the alternative reading is noted in Design Notes).
- No in-match diplomacy / alliance changes: the mask is set at scenario-apply and immutable for the match (1.0, documented). No lobby/setup UI for teams (Story 9.14/9.15).
- No `CanonicalModelHash` change and no `hero-start-state.golden.txt` re-record — the alliance mask folds into `SimChecksum` only this story (the match-start handshake wiring is Story 9.14).
- No second win-evaluation path: presentation holds no win math; do not revive `CheckWinCondition`.
- No `float`→`int` truncation and no string formatting inside the tick.

## I/O & Edge-Case Matrix

| Scenario | Input / State | Expected Output / Behavior | Error Handling |
|----------|--------------|---------------------------|----------------|
| 3-FFA elimination (built-in) | P1 loses all buildings at tick T (past grace); P2, P3 alive | `Verdict[P1]=LOST` at T; match continues; no game-over yet | No error |
| 3-FFA → last standing | continuing above, P2 later loses all buildings; only P3 live | `Verdict[P3]=WON`, `Verdict[P2]=LOST`; `ShowGameOver(3)` | No error |
| 2v2 team wipe (allied mask) | teams {P1,P2} vs {P3,P4}; P3 & P4 both eliminated | both `LOST`; `Verdict[P1]=Verdict[P2]=WON` (whole team) | No error |
| 2-faction double-elim parity | P1 & P2 both zero-buildings same tick | `Verdict[P2]=WON`, `Verdict[P1]=LOST` (7.11 tie-break preserved) | No error |
| KotH per-team (3 factions) | team {P1,P2} solely holds region N contiguous ticks | team counter reaches N → `Verdict[P1]=Verdict[P2]=WON`, `Verdict[P3]=LOST` | undefined region_id → 7.11 fallback |
| KotH contested by two teams | team A unit and team B unit both in region same tick | no team solely holds → all team counters reset to 0 | No error |
| Timed Survival (3 factions) | designated P2 alive at deadline; teams-of-1 | `Verdict[P2]=WON`, `Verdict[P1]=Verdict[P3]=LOST` | survive_ticks ≤ 0 → 7.11 reject |
| Survival designate dies early | designated P2 eliminated before deadline (past grace); P1,P3 alive | `Verdict[P2]=LOST`; match continues; P1 vs P3 to last-standing | No error |
| Assassination (4 FFA) | P1's designated leader dies; P2,P3,P4 alive | `Verdict[P1]=LOST` at death tick; others eliminated by wipeout; last team wins | -1 unresolved target → 7.11 grace-gated owner loss |
| Local player eliminated | `LocalFaction=P1` latches LOST, match not resolved | `FogBridge.RevealAll=true` + defeat banner; ticking continues | No error |
| 4-faction replay determinism | same seeded 4-faction scenario + command stream, two runs | byte-identical final `SimChecksum` (`AlgoVersion==20`) | No error |
| Null alliance store (legacy call site) | `SimChecksum.Compute(..., alliances: null)` | folds byte-identically to a default-FFA store | No error |

## Design Notes

**Scope decision — generalize the resolver, keep single-designation presets (evidence-backed):** AC3 lists "KotH cumulative time per team, Assassination per-team leader, etc." as illustrations of the last-team-standing principle. Two readings exist: (A) generalize the *resolution machinery* (elimination + alliance mask + last-team-standing) while the preset data model keeps its single designation, and (B) expand every preset to per-faction multi-target designation (each of N factions gets its own protected leader/landmark/survivor). Reading A is **selected** by the surrounding intent, not chosen arbitrarily: the story "Depends on: 7.11" only; the gap-closure note flags "⚑ One fold" (a determinism-scoped story about the alliance mask, not a preset data-model expansion); its stated purpose is closing "3+ player matches cannot **conclude**" (elimination + last-team-standing), not objective richness; and reading B requires either the Story 7.13 instance-designation vocabulary (7.11's own Block-If territory) or a spec/picker/validator/serializer expansion inconsistent with "one fold." Under A, Assassination/Landmark designate one faction's protected asset (death → that team loses) and all other factions are eliminated by total wipeout, resolving by last-team-standing — which concludes correctly for N factions. Full symmetric multi-leader FFA assassination is the documented boundary (Never), deferred to the designation-vocabulary work.

**Unified per-tick resolution (the heart):** after advancing 7.11 counters, in this order — (1) **loss pass:** for each active `VERDICT_NONE` faction, if its preset loss predicate holds (built-in: no alive assets of the relevant kind; survival: designated faction eliminated; assassination/landmark: target dead or unresolved; plus, for the non-designated factions of asymmetric presets, total wipeout), latch `VERDICT_LOST` per the 7.11 grace rules (loss-by-absence grace-gated; resolved-target death ungated). (2) **positive-objective win:** if a KotH team reached `hold_ticks` or a Survival team reached its deadline alive, latch `VERDICT_WON` for that team and `VERDICT_LOST` for all others; done. (3) **last-team-standing:** live teams = distinct `TeamOf(f)` over active factions with `VERDICT_NONE`. If exactly one live team AND ≥1 faction already `LOST`, latch `VERDICT_WON` for that team's live factions. If **zero** live teams (simultaneous elimination of the last ≥2 teams this tick), the highest-slot faction eliminated this tick (and its team) wins instead — this reproduces the 7.11 P1+P2 double-elim→Player2 tie-break (Player2 is the higher slot). In FFA (teams-of-1, the default) every "team" is one faction, so the whole model degenerates to last-faction-standing and matches 7.11 exactly in the 2-faction case.

**Team-aware KotH:** `UpdateKothCounters` aggregates region presence by `TeamOf`; advance the counter only when exactly one team is present (store the accruing count on the team's representative = lowest-slot member so the fold stays per-faction and contiguous team holding survives an individual ally leaving), reset all others to 0. Neutral is excluded (not in `ActiveFactions`) — a Neutral bystander neither holds nor contests, per 7.11.

**Alliance store placement (dedicated, not co-located in `WinStateStore`):** allied **vision** and allied **targeting** (Story 9.14) will query alliances outside the win system, so the mask is a first-class store any system can read (`AreAllied`), not a field of the win-runtime store. It is immutable per match in 1.0; folding a static array into the per-tick `SimChecksum` is the mechanism that forces peers to agree on teams (a peer with a different mask resolves victory differently → must desync detectably). Mirror 7.11's null≡empty fold discipline exactly: a null `AllianceStore` folds byte-identically to a default-FFA store so every legacy `Compute` call site stays consistent.

**Fold discipline (highest risk, mirrors 7.11):** one `AlgoVersion` bump (19→20) → re-baseline the per-tick world goldens (the `ExpectedV19Hash` in `SimChecksumCoverageGuardTest` has no record hook — copy the new hex from the failure message and rename to v20). The alliance fold must be **omit-neutral for existing default (FFA) scenarios apart from the version bump** — but note every existing scenario is FFA, so all world goldens WILL move (the fold adds a `Mix` per active faction); that is the expected, disclosed churn. `hero-start-state.golden.txt` must NOT move (no `CanonicalModelHash` change).
